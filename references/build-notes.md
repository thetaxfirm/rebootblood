## EBOO Safety & Contraindications (REQUIRED SECTION)
- Framing: supportive wellness service, NOT a substitute for emergency care or your primary medical team
- Absolute contraindication: significant G6PD deficiency (favism) — hemolysis risk
- Relative (need clearance): bleeding/coagulation disorders or active bleeding risk; thrombocytopenia; recent MI; hemorrhagic stroke/recent major cerebrovascular event; uncontrolled hyperthyroidism; pregnancy (esp. 1st trimester); alcohol intoxication
- Route notes: safety depends on route/dose/protocol; inhaled ozone NOT therapeutic & harmful to lungs
- Screening: history, meds/supplements, prior reactions, vitals, G6PD lab when appropriate
- Short-term effects: temporary fatigue/malaise, headache, lightheadedness; possible nausea; local site irritation
- Seek urgent care: severe SOB, chest pain, unresolved fainting, severe IV-site swelling/redness, any severe/abnormal symptom
